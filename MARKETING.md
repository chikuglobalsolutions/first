## 0. Operating rules for the daily routine (READ FIRST)

This routine grows the businesses with **first-party, opt-in marketing** — content
the owner publishes on channels they control, and inbound funnels that capture
people who choose to hear from us.

**What this routine does NOT do — and why:**

- ❌ **No scraping email addresses off Instagram (or any platform).** Instagram's
  Terms of Service prohibit automated data collection, and harvesting personal
  contact details for outreach is exactly what anti-spam law (US CAN-SPAM, EU/UK
  GDPR & PECR, CASL in Canada) is written to punish. It also gets accounts and
  domains banned, which destroys the very channels we need to grow.
- ❌ **No unsolicited cold email/DM blasts to people who never asked to hear from us.**
  This tanks sender reputation, kills deliverability for the real list, and is the
  fastest way to a Stripe/ESP shutdown.

**What it does instead (legal, durable, actually converts):**

- ✅ **First-party content** — posts, threads, and short-form scripts the owner
  publishes on the brand's own IG / X / LinkedIn / TikTok / Reddit accounts.
- ✅ **Opt-in list building** — lead magnets + a signup form so people volunteer
  their email; then value-first email sequences (fully CAN-SPAM/GDPR compliant).
- ✅ **Competitor & niche research** — study high-follower creators in each niche
  for *what content works*, hooks, and positioning (observing public content is
  fine; extracting their audience's contacts is not).
- ✅ **Warm outreach done right** — for the B2B agency (AI Systems), personalized
  1:1 outreach to a *small, hand-qualified* list of businesses, with real opt-out
  and honest identification, is legitimate. Bulk-scraped blasts are not.

If a future run is tempted to add a scraper or a bulk-blast, stop and re-read this
section. The boundary is deliberate.

## 2. The legit growth engine (what "daily" means now)

Each daily run should do a subset of these, and log it in the tracker (§4):

1. **Research** one high-performing creator/brand in one niche and note *what content
   format & hooks* are working (public observation only — no contact extraction).
2. **Produce** a batch of ready-to-publish content for one or more brands
   (saved under `marketing/content-YYYY-MM-DD.md`).
3. **Improve** one on-site conversion asset (landing copy, a lead magnet, SEO meta,
   the QR generator's share loop) via a normal PR.
4. **Update** this file with what was learned and what to do next.

The owner's job each day: copy/paste the day's content to the brand accounts, and
unblock the items in §5.

## 3. Per-brand positioning & content angles

### PromptEmpire (AI prompt packs)
- **Buyer:** freelancers, solopreneurs, small-agency owners who want ChatGPT to
  produce business output without prompt-engineering skill.
- **Winning angle:** "Stop staring at a blank ChatGPT box." Show a before/after of a
  vague prompt vs. one of the pack's prompts. Proof > hype.
- **Channels:** X threads, LinkedIn carousels, r/ChatGPT / r/Entrepreneur (value-first,
  soft link in comments per subreddit rules).

### Chiku Fit (bulking guides)
- **Buyer:** skinny/"hardgainer" guys 18–30 trying to put on size on a budget.
- **Winning angle:** "Bulking is expensive" is the objection — the "Broke Bulk" meal
  plan is the wedge. Cheap-calorie tips, grocery hauls, cheap high-protein meals.
- **Channels:** IG Reels + TikTok (grocery/meal short-form), r/Gainit, r/bodybuilding.

### AI Systems (Dental AI Agency)
- **Buyer:** dental practice owners / office managers losing revenue to missed calls
  and no-shows.
- **Winning angle:** quantify leaked revenue (missed calls × avg patient value). ROI,
  not tech. Case-study-style LinkedIn posts.
- **Outreach:** legit = small, hand-picked list, personalized, with opt-out. Not bulk.

### QR Generator (qrflow)
- **Buyer:** small businesses, event organizers, restaurants wanting trackable QR codes.
- **Winning angle:** free tool with analytics the free competitors don't give →
  product-led growth. Every generated code is a marketing surface.
- **Growth loop:** SEO ("free QR code generator with analytics"), and a subtle
  "made with" attribution on free-tier codes.
