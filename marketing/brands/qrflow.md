# Brand Profile — qrflow (QR code generator)

- **What it is:** The original Chiku product — a QR code generator with scan tracking and
  analytics. Dynamic QR codes, device breakdown, scan logs.
- **Promise:** Make a QR code in seconds, then actually see who scans it.
- **Pricing (Stripe subscription, from `src/lib/stripe.ts`):**

| Plan | Price | QR limit | Analytics | Custom colors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 5 | — | — |
| Pro | **$9/mo** | 500 | ✅ | ✅ |
| Business | **$29/mo** | Unlimited | ✅ | ✅ |

- **Try / buy:** `/generate` (make one), `/dashboard` (manage + analytics). Upgrade via Stripe checkout.

## Audience
SMB marketers, restaurants/menus, event organizers, creators, real-estate signage, retail/packaging
— anyone putting a QR in the physical world who wants to measure it.

## Content pillars
1. **"Static QR is a dead end"** — dynamic QR + analytics vs. a dumb image.
2. **Use-case spotlights** — menu, flyer, business card, product packaging, event.
3. **Scan-data insights** — what device/time data tells you about a campaign.
4. **Speed/ease** — "QR + tracking in under 60 seconds".
5. **Free-tier on-ramp** — start free (5 codes), upgrade when you scale.

## Voice
Practical, marketer-to-marketer, quick wins. Show the dashboard.

"Make a tracked QR free → /generate" then "Need more than 5? Pro is $9/mo → upgrade".
Lead-gen: "1 free dynamic QR + a sample scan report → [opt-in]".
