# Brand Profile — Chiku AI Systems

- **What it is:** Done-for-you AI growth systems for any business. 6 productized systems mapped
  across 12 industries. Lead via the intake form on `/ai-systems`, then a sales call.
- **Promise:** Plug AI into the leaky parts of a business (lead follow-up, reviews, booking,
  sales, reactivation, reporting) without hiring.
- **Price:** From **$1,497 setup**. (High-ticket → goal of content is booked strategy calls, not
  direct checkout.)

## The 6 systems
| System | One-liner | Headline outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Lead Follow-Up Engine | Instant personalized response to every lead, 24/7, 14-day nurture | +45% lead-to-booking, 2-min response |
| Review Generation System | Automated post-service review requests, route happy→public / unhappy→private | 3–5× reviews in 90 days |
| Booking Optimization | Smart reminders + waitlist auto-fill | −60% no-shows, ~$8K/mo recovered |
| AI Sales Assistant | Always-on FAQ/qualify/quote/book across site + DMs | 70–80% inquiries handled autonomously |
| Reactivation Campaigns | AI win-back sequences to dormant lists | 3–8× vs cold, $0 ad spend |
| Reporting & Insights | Weekly cross-tool summary with action items | 1 source of truth, 3–5 insights/wk |

3. **Industry-specific** — same system, framed for dentists vs. HVAC vs. law firms.
4. **Before/after workflow** — what a day looks like with the system running.
5. **Build-vs-hire math** — system vs. a $60K/yr SDR.
