## Audience
Skinny beginners with no lifting experience · limited budgets · gym members with barbell/dumbbell
access · people who struggle to eat enough · people who need structure over motivation.

## Founder story (use it — it's the differentiator)
Aku was the skinny guy who couldn't gain weight despite trying everything. The fix was
"embarrassingly simple": a real, **tracked** calorie surplus (a number, not vibes) + double-
progression overload (add reps, then add weight, reset reps). Philosophy: *"structured enough to
follow without thinking, cheap enough to actually do, and honest enough to work."*

## Content pillars

2. **Embarrassingly-simple truths** — surplus math, double progression.

4. **Founder transformation / honesty** — no BS, no supplements upsell.
