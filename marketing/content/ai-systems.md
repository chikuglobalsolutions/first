## LinkedIn posts (owner-targeted)

**1. The lost-lead math**
> Most local businesses lose 40–60% of leads for one boring reason: nobody follows up fast enough.
>
> A lead comes in at 8pm. Your team sees it at 9am. By then half have called a competitor who answered first.
>
> We build an AI follow-up engine that responds in under 2 minutes, 24/7, personalized to what they asked.
> One client went from a leaky funnel to +45% lead-to-booking. No new staff.
>
> If you're a [dental / law / home-services] owner losing leads after hours, that's the gap to close first.

**2. Reviews compound (for or against you)**
> Your happy customers stay silent. Your one upset customer writes a novel. Over years, that math buries you.
>
> Fix: ask every happy customer at the *right* moment (24–72h post-service), route the unhappy ones to a
> private channel before they go public. Clients 3–5× their Google reviews in 90 days doing exactly this.
>
> Reviews drive 60%+ of new-customer trust. This is the cheapest lever most owners ignore.

**3. No-show post (very tangible)**
> No-shows quietly kill 15–25% of revenue in appointment businesses.
> Smart reminders at 24h / 4h / 30min + an AI waitlist that fills cancellations in 60 seconds =
> -60% no-shows and ~$8K/mo recovered for a typical practice.
> You already did the marketing to book them. Don't lose them at the door.
