## Sending notes

- **Engine:** Resend (SMTP) is already configured for NextAuth in this codebase. The same
  Resend client can send these via the Resend API. Wire up via a `marketing/nurture-send.ts`
  script or wire it into the `/api/leads` route to enqueue email 1 immediately.
- **Time zones:** send email 1 instantly, all others at 8:30am recipient TZ (use Resend's
  `scheduled_at` parameter).
- **Suppression:** when someone buys (Stripe webhook), remove them from the nurture sequence.
  Wire this in `src/app/api/stripe/webhook/route.ts` — on `checkout.session.completed`,
  upsert a suppression record by email.
- **Reply tracking:** anyone who replies to any nurture email gets a manual touch within 24h.
  Replies are leads.
