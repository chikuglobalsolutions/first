# Marketing Playbook

Tactics, templates, and learnings that grow over time. The daily routine appends here whenever it
finds a better way to do something. Treat everything unmarked as a **hypothesis** until measured.

## Content principles (Chiku voice)
- Lead with the **problem the reader already feels** ("staring at a blank screen", "you're not
  eating enough", "leads going to voicemail"). Specific > clever.
- Plain, honest, founder-to-peer tone. No hype words ("revolutionary", "10x guru"). ADHD-aware:
  short, scannable, one clear action.
- Always end with **one** CTA and a real link.
- Buy-once / no-subscription is a selling point for the digital products — say it.

## Post structure that works (default template)
1. **Hook** (1 line, pattern-interrupt or painful truth)
2. **Body** (2–5 short lines or a tight list; concrete, no fluff)
3. **CTA** (one ask + link)
4. **Hashtags** (3–6, niche not generic)

## Lead-magnet → opt-in funnel (the legitimate growth loop)
1. Free, genuinely useful asset (calculator, mini-pack, checklist) gated behind email opt-in.
2. Opt-in lands them in a Mailchimp audience (per brand).
3. Short welcome sequence (3 emails) delivering value → soft pitch to the paid offer.
4. Ongoing newsletter keeps the list warm. **All opt-in. No scraping, ever.**

### Lead-magnet ideas by brand
- **PromptEmpire:** "10 free cold-DM prompts that book calls" (teaser for the 500-pack).
- **Chiku Fit:** "Skinny-guy calorie calculator + 3 starter workouts" (teaser for 8-Week program).
- **Chiku AI Systems:** "No-show cost calculator" / "Lead-response-time scorecard" (teaser for a strategy call).
- **qrflow:** "1 free dynamic QR + scan report" (teaser for Pro).

## Platform notes
- **X/Threads:** hook must stand alone; thread for depth; 1–2 hashtags max.
- **LinkedIn:** longer-form, story + lesson; best for AI Systems (B2B) and PromptEmpire (founders).
- **Instagram/TikTok:** visual-first; caption hook in first line; carousel or short video script.
- **Email:** subject line is 80% of the open; test 2 per send.
