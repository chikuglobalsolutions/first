# Cold email sequence library

> Three sequences. Each one is touch 1 + touch 2 + touch 3 + breakup. Pre-personalized for
> a specific audience. Drop into your inbox tool of choice (Smartlead, Lemlist, Instantly).
> Personalize the [BLANK]s before sending. Do not send Plus-AI-flavored output as-is.

## Send checklist (every batch)

- [ ] List is < 50 recipients per send (preserve sender rep)
- [ ] Each row has a personalization hook in `[SPECIFIC X]`
- [ ] Sending domain has had 14+ days of warmup
- [ ] Sent between Tue–Thu, 8:30–11am recipient TZ
- [ ] Replies routed to your real inbox, not a noreply
- [ ] Logged in `marketing/_LOG.md` after sending

## Don't send if...

- You couldn't find a real personalization hook (mass-flatter emails tank reputation)
- The recipient already bought (check Stripe before each batch)
- You sent them another email in the last 7 days
- Their domain is on your suppression list
