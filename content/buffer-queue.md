# Buffer Bulk-Upload Content Queue

A paste-ready posting queue for all four Chiku Global Solutions brands. Every post below is
self-contained (works on X, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Threads), ends on a natural CTA, and
carries 3–5 hashtags.

## How to use this

1. Open **Buffer** and use the **bulk / queue composer** (the multi-post paste box).
2. **Paste one post per entry.** Each numbered block below is a single post — copy the post
   text (not the number label) into its own slot.
3. **Posts are grouped by brand** so you can assign each group to the right
   channel/queue. Do PromptEmpire first, assign it to the PromptEmpire channels, then move on
   to the next brand.
4. **Links:** these are written link-free so they read naturally. Add the correct link
   per your Buffer setup — either in the post where it says "link in bio," via Buffer's
   link field, or a pinned/first-comment link. Point every CTA to the right destination:
   - PromptEmpire → `chikuglobalsolutions.com/promptempire` (free 10-prompt signup)
   - Chiku Fit → `/chiku-fit` (free budget grocery list signup)
   - Chiku AI Systems → `/ai-systems` (free 12-point checklist signup)
   - QRFlow → `/generate` (free trackable code)
5. **Keep it organic.** These are public, opt-in posts. No spam, no cold DMs, no scraping —
   only ever invite people to sign up for the free lead magnet on the site.

## Suggested weekly cadence

A sustainable 3–5 posts per week per brand. Spread across the week so no single day is
overloaded, and rotate through each brand's 10 posts (roughly a 2–3 week supply before you
refresh or reuse).

Tips for keeping it sustainable:
- Bulk-load a full week (or two) into Buffer in one sitting, then let the queue run.
- B2B brands (Chiku AI Systems, QRFlow) tend to do best on weekday business hours; the
  consumer brands (PromptEmpire, Chiku Fit) hold up well on evenings and weekends — adjust
  Buffer's posting times per channel accordingly.
- When a brand's 10 posts run out, lightly reword the best performers and requeue rather than
  scrambling for all-new copy.
