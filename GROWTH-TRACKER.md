## Operating policy for this routine (read this first)

This is an **unattended daily routine**. To keep the businesses safe and the accounts alive:

- ❌ **No scraping** of Instagram or any platform (breaks their ToS, risks bans).
- ❌ **No harvesting** personal/creator emails for unsolicited outreach (CAN-SPAM / GDPR / spam-trap risk).
- ❌ **No auto-publishing** posts, ads, or emails to the public. The routine prepares **drafts**; a human approves before anything goes live.
- ✅ **Yes** to: market research, content drafting, tracking, your own **opt-in** email list (Mailchimp), SEO, and partnership *shortlists* (publicly-listed business emails only, contacted manually and sparingly, always with an opt-out).

If you want creator/influencer partnerships, the compliant path is: find creators whose bio
*publicly lists a "for collabs" business email*, shortlist them here, and send a small number
of **personalised** pitches by hand — not a daily automated blast. I can build that shortlist;
I won't auto-send it.
