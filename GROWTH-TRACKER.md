## ⛔ Boundary: what this routine will NOT do

A previous version of the brief asked to scrape Instagram emails daily and cold-blast them.
That is **off the table** and here's the standing reason so it doesn't get re-added:

1. **Instagram scraping breaks their ToS** → accounts/IPs get banned, including your real brand accounts.
2. **Cold-emailing scraped contacts = spam** under CAN-SPAM / GDPR → legal exposure.
3. **It destroys your own deliverability** → once the domain is flagged, Stripe receipts,
   NextAuth magic-links, and agency intake emails all start hitting spam folders. You'd
   break the money machine to chase cold leads.

**Compliant equivalent that actually works:** publish content where the niche already gathers,
build an opt-in list (Mailchimp double opt-in), and do *personalized, manual* partnership
outreach to creators via the business-inquiry channels they themselves publish. No bulk, no scrape.
