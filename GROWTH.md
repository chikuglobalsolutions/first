## ⚠️ Read this first — what this system does and does NOT do

The original ask was: *"email scrape a new Instagram page each day, make sure creators
have 10k+ followers in similar niches, then market them all."*

**We do not run that.** Scraping emails from Instagram and cold-emailing them is:
- A violation of Instagram's Terms of Service (automated data extraction).
- Unsolicited commercial email — regulated by **CAN-SPAM (US)**, **CASL (Canada)**,
  and **GDPR/PECR (EU/UK)**. Fines run into the thousands *per message*.
- **Actively harmful to the business:** spam complaints get the sending domain
  blacklisted, which destroys email deliverability for real, paying customers and
  for Stripe/NextAuth transactional email.

Instead we grow the four brands with methods that compound and don't put the domain
at risk. Everything below is built on **consent-based** channels: organic content,
opt-in email lists, and *legitimate* creator partnerships (public DMs / documented
outreach to people who publish partnership contact info themselves).

If you want influencer collaborations, the right play is **manual, opt-in outreach**
to a small shortlist of creators who list a business/collab email in their bio —
tracked in the table below — not automated scraping. See "Creator partnerships (compliant)."

## The daily growth loop (compliant, repeatable)

Run this each day instead of the scrape. Fill one row in the **Daily Tracker** below.

1. **Publish 1 piece of organic content per brand** (draft bank below — never post empty).
2. **Engage, don't scrape:** reply to comments/DMs on each brand's own posts. This is
   what actually converts and it's 100% within-ToS.
3. **Grow the opt-in list:** every piece of content drives to a lead magnet
   (free prompt sample / free workout PDF / free AI audit / free QR + analytics trial).
   Only email people who opt in. Mailchimp (connected) manages this.
4. **Creator partnerships (compliant):** if pursuing collabs, add **max 3** creators/day
   to the shortlist table — only ones who *publish* a collab/business email in their own
   bio — and log a *manual, personalized* outreach. No bulk sends, no scraping.
5. **Measure:** log follows/signups/sales deltas per brand so we double down on winners.
